Privacy

Updated November 11, 2026

SD Storefront is run by Lance Cowling. This notice explains how information submitted for a business website concept is used.

What we collect

Our request forms ask for your name, email, business name, business links, service area and other business details you choose to provide. The Facebook request form may also ask whether you make website decisions and understand the advertised price. Do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

How we use it

We use request information to assess whether the one-page website offer fits, prepare and review a requested concept, contact you about that request and answer questions. We may consult the public business pages you identify. We do not sell your request details.

Services involved

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Request forms are hosted by Google or Meta, depending on where you submit. Email is handled through Gmail. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms. Our landing page does not contain an advertising pixel or an embedded tracking script. The hosting provider and form services may collect technical information under their own policies.

Your choices

A concept request does not create a paid subscription. You can ask us to stop following up, correct your information, or remove your request details and concept by emailing sandiegostorefront@gmail.com. We review those requests and may need to confirm that the information belongs to you. Separate records held by Google or Meta are subject to their own controls.
