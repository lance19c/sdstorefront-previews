Questions you may have

Straight answers, including the ones about e-mail.

Why did I get an e-mail from you?

We look at businesses in San Diego County using public information, such as map listings and the business's own website. If yours looked like it could use a clearer website, we may have built a concept for it and written to you once.

I do not want to hear from you.

Reply "no thanks" to our e-mail, or write to sandiegostorefront@gmail.com, and we will not contact you or your domain again.

Can you take down the concept page you built?

Yes. Write to us and it comes down within one business day. Every concept page also says how to ask.

Is the concept page about my business accurate?

It is built from public listings and may be out of date or incomplete. It is only a starting point, and you decide what is true and what goes on your real site.

Who reads my message and checks the sample?

A person (Lance) reviews each message and each sample before it is sent. We use software to read public web pages, which is the visitor described in the next question.

What is the "SDStorefrontHarbor" visitor in my website logs?

That is our reader. It fetches only public pages, reads a site's robots.txt first, goes slowly, and shows this page's address in its user-agent so you can find us.
