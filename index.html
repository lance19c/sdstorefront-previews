A few fair questions.

Is the sample really free?

Yes. No payment card is needed, and requesting a sample does not enroll you in the monthly plan.

What if I only have Facebook or Instagram?

You can still request a concept. Add your business profile link, or enter “none” and describe your business in the link field. We will clarify anything missing before building.

How soon can I see a sample?

We review requests and confirm timing by email. We agree a launch date only after you have approved the content and scope.

Does the website guarantee more customers?

No. A clear website gives people a place to learn about your business and contact you. Sales also depend on demand, traffic, your offer and how enquiries are handled.

Does this include an online store or a complex booking system?

This offer is for a simple one-page business website. Tell us what you need; we confirm whether it fits before you pay. Custom systems are not included in the advertised plan.
